# My Account Page

A self-service account center for tenants: balance, plan, and top-up history.

## Definitions

Balance = cash wallet; subscription credits are displayed separately and are not combined.

## How to Check When Numbers Do Not Match

- 1. The balance did not change after a top-up: The payment callback may be delayed by a few minutes; if the funds still have not been credited after the timeout, verify the payment using the payment reference number.
- The balance and usage do not match: First confirm the reporting timestamp; usage deductions are processed in real time.
