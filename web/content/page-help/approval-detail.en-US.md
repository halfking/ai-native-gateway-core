# Approval Details Page

The complete record of a single approval: trigger reason, linked request, current status, and available actions.

## Explanation of Terms

Status transition = Pending Approval → Approved/Rejected; action history is traceable.

## How to Self-Check When Figures Do Not Match

- 1. The change does not take effect after approval: There may be a delay of a few seconds; if it takes more than one minute, verify it on the corresponding feature page.
- 2. The request content is not visible in the details: Content summaries are redacted based on permissions, and higher permissions are required to view the full content.
