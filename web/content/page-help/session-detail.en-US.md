# Session Details Page Data Guide

This page provides an overview of an entire conversation: what it covered, how many turns it had, and what was asked and answered in each turn, helping administrators understand the full background and history of an individual service interaction.

## What You Can See on This Page

- **Conversation summary bar**: A summary of the conversation topic, the participants, the number of turns, and whether it is still in progress.
- **Turn timeline**: Each question-and-answer exchange in chronological order, which can be expanded turn by turn to view the full content.
- **Turn highlights**: Condensed summaries of important turns, making it easy to quickly locate key points.

## Definitions and Counting Rules

- A **session** refers to a single continuous conversation; all figures on the page count only that session and are not aggregated across sessions.
- The **turn count** is based on alternating questions and answers: each user question starts a new turn, and the answer belongs to that same turn regardless of its length.
- The **summary** is automatically generated by the system from the entire conversation. If the session is still in progress, the summary may lag behind the latest turns; use the “Generation Time” as the reference.
- The **displayed time** is the time recorded by the server. When viewing across time zones, use the unified system time based on UTC date boundaries; no local time zone conversion is applied.

## What to Check When Numbers Do Not Match

- **The turn count does not match the list page**: Filters may be active on the list page. Clear all filters and compare again.
- **The summary does not reflect the latest conversation**: Summaries are generated periodically while a session is still in progress. Wait a few minutes, refresh the page, and check again; a “Generation Time” earlier than the last turn is normal.
- **A turn cannot be opened**: The turn may have been deleted after exceeding the storage retention period. It may still appear in the list, but its content is no longer retained, in accordance with the established retention policy.
- **Both views show a session with the same title**: Use the session ID to determine whether they are the same session. Similar titles do not mean they are the same conversation.
