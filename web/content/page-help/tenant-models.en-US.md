# Standard Model Page

Model catalog from the tenant’s perspective: the models and prices available to this tenant.

## Pricing Definitions

Price = credits per 1 million Tokens; includes four items: input, output, cache reads, and cache writes.

## How to Self-Check When Numbers Do Not Match

- 1. A model is not shown: the administrator has not made it available to this tenant; contact the administrator to enable it.
- The price differs from the admin console: the admin console shows the USD cost price, while the tenant side shows the selling price in credits. These are two separate pricing systems.
