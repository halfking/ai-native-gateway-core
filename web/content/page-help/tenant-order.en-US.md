# Order Details Page

Complete information about an order: what was purchased, how much was paid, and the current status.

## Definitions

Order status flow = Awaiting payment → Paid → Completed; refunds are returned via the original payment method.

## How to self-check when the numbers do not match

- 1. Payment succeeded, but the order has not updated: The callback is delayed; refresh the page or wait a little, then verify using the payment transaction number.
- The order amount differs from the package price: Discounts or reductions in effect at the time are included and itemized in the details.
