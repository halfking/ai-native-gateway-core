# Plans and Top-up Page

Tenants can make self-service purchases: monthly plan tiers and booster pack top-ups.

## Quota Definitions

Monthly plan = subscription credits that reset each month; booster pack = one-time credits that remain available until used up.

## How to Self-Check When Numbers Don't Match

- 1. Purchase completed but credits not received: payment callbacks may be delayed by a few minutes; if the delay exceeds the expected window, verify the transaction using the order number.
- 2. Deduction order for plans and booster packs: deduct from the plan's included subscription credits first, then the booster pack credits, and finally the wallet balance.
