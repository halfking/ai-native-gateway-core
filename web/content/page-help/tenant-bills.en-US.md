# Monthly Billing Page

Tenant monthly billing: one bill per month, locked after issuance.

## Billing Basis

Bills are issued by calendar month (UTC), and the previous month’s bill is issued the next day. Once issued, a bill cannot be changed.

## How to Reconcile Discrepancies

- 1. No bill is visible for the current month: The current month’s bill is not issued until the next month. For current-month usage, see “My Usage.”
- The bill amount differs from the real-time dashboard: The bill includes all approved adjustments as of the time it was issued. The bill is the source of truth.
