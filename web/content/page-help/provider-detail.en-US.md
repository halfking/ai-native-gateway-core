# Vendor Details Page

A complete vendor profile: credential list, model catalog, health history, and usage share.

## Calculation Notes

Credential health and model availability are assessed separately; usage share is calculated based on the selected time window.

## How to Self-Check When Numbers Do Not Match

- 1.The model list differs from the catalog page: the details page includes models whose routes are not live, while the catalog page shows only models available for purchase.
- Total usage share ≠100%: long-tail models are grouped under "Other".
