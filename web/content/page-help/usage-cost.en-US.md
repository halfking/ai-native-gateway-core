# Usage Cost Page

Usage analysis from a cost perspective: who is spending the most and where the money is going.

## Calculation Basis

Cost = upstream cost in USD; when broken down by dimension, uncategorized items are listed separately.

## How to Self-Check When the Numbers Do Not Match

- 1. Different from the reconciliation report: Reconciliation uses frozen daily snapshots; this page aggregates in real time and includes same-day data that has not yet been billed.
- Breakdown total ≠ total usage: Usage with a missing dimension is included under "Uncategorized".
