# Subscription Tier Page

Definition and management of monthly package tiers: the quota included in each tier and its price.

## Definitions

Tier changes apply to new subscriptions; existing subscriptions continue under the tier purchased.

## How to Check for Discrepancies

- 1.The price changed, but existing customers did not: existing subscriptions remain on their original tier and move to the new tier only when they renew at expiration.
- The tier quota does not match the package on the marketing page: configure both from the same source; if they diverge, check the sync job first.
