## 5. 会话侧 ⇄ 请求级（同会话集）—— 判定「会话侧是否重复计算」

```sql
WITH u AS (SELECT DISTINCT ON (session_id, tenant_id) session_id, tenant_id, is_probe, owner_user, client_type, title
  FROM sessions ORDER BY session_id, tenant_id, partition_date DESC),
kept AS (SELECT ss.session_key, ss.tenant_id, ss.request_count, ss.total_prompt_tokens, ss.total_completion_tokens, ss.total_cost_usd
  FROM session_summaries ss
  JOIN u s ON s.session_id = ss.session_key AND s.tenant_id = ss.tenant_id
  LEFT JOIN session_dim sd ON sd.session_key = ss.session_key AND sd.tenant_id = ss.tenant_id
  WHERE ss.first_request_at >= TIMESTAMPTZ '2026-11-17 00:00+08' AND ss.first_request_at < TIMESTAMPTZ '2026-11-25 00:00+08'
    AND NULLIF(COALESCE(s.owner_user, sd.api_key_owner_user),'') IS NOT NULL
    AND ( COALESCE(ss.total_cost_usd,0) > 0
          OR ( NOT COALESCE(s.is_probe,false)
               AND NOT session_analytics_is_probe(COALESCE(s.owner_user, sd.api_key_owner_user), s.client_type, s.title) ) )),
```

## 12. 积分：NULL 还是 0（决定结论方向）

