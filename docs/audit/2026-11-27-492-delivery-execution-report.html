📊 迁移内容概览

创建的表

表名 用途 关键列
channels 渠道商主表 id, name, slug, created_at
customers 客户主表 id, name, created_at
channel_users 渠道商用户 id, channel_id, user_id
channels_customers 渠道-客户关联 channel_id, customer_id

  • 核心契约: OwnsCustomer(channel, customer) - 渠道商只能看自己名下客户
