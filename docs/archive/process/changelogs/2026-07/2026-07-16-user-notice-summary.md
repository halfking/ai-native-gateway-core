# User notice summary on public portal

Surface the "user notice" summary on the public distribution portal instead of
leaving it at the bottom of the landing page. The summary appears as a
collapsible block on `/download` and on the trial activation step of
`/activate`, with a link to the full `/user-agreement.html`. A new
"User Notice" item is added to the public portal top navigation so users can
open the agreement from anywhere on the distribution site. The summary and
four section titles are added to `zh-CN` and `en-US` locale bundles; other
locales fall back to English.
